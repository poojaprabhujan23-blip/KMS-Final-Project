KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (KMS)

Project Overview

A Knowledge Management System (KMS) is a centralized platform developed to simplify the process of creating, storing, organizing, managing, and sharing information. It enables users to access relevant knowledge, retrieve important resources, and maintain information systematically. The system aims to improve knowledge accessibility, information sharing, collaboration, and overall productivity through efficient knowledge management.

Problem Being Solved

Traditional methods of managing information often make it difficult to store, organize, search, and retrieve knowledge efficiently. This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform where users can securely manage, access, and share information, reducing information duplication and improving accessibility.

Key Contributions & Architecture

  • Centralized Knowledge Repository: Unified storage and systematic management of structured information.
  • Resource Lifecycle: Streamlined creation, uploading, categorization, and editing of knowledge resources.
  • Fast Resource Retrieval: Rapid search indexing allowing quick search and retrieval of stored resources.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Accessible, responsive design enhancing knowledge sharing across devices.
  • Efficient Collaboration: Eliminates information redundancy and boosts productivity.

Backend Layer (Server & API)

Database Layer (Data Persistence)
